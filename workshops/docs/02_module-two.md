# Point-In-Time Illuminance

This module covers the workflow for conducting point-in-time illuminance simulations. It introduces key concepts relating to light and materiality. Illuminance is the measure of how much incident light or the total luminous flux reaches a surface. This accounts for all light arriving at the observed surface including light that gets reflected off other surfaces.
